## Attribution

If you use the data or the figures, CC BY 4.0 asks that you credit the source,
link to the license, and say whether you changed anything. Citing the paper
satisfies this:

> S. Hattori, T. Hiraki, Y. Itoh, and Y. Hiroi, "A cone-opponent amplitude
> budget for imperceptible color vibration on head-mounted displays."

The MIT license asks only that its notice travel with the code.
